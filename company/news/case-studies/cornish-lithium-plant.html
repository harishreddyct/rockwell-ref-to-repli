The Challenge

Cornish Lithium set out to prove that lithium could be sustainably extracted from mica in Cornish granite — a novel approach requiring precise, reliable process control.

The Solution

A PlantPAx distributed control system provided the visibility and control needed to run the demonstration plant reliably and gather the data to validate the process.

The Results

The project demonstrated a repeatable, sustainable processing approach and laid the foundation for scaling battery-metal production in the UK.
